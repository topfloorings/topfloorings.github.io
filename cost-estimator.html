What affects the cost

  • Material type. Laminate, SPC vinyl, engineered hardwood and solid hardwood all have different prices per sq ft, and labour rates differ too.
  • Waste. Cuts and mistakes mean you buy extra. 10% is common for straight installs. Read how much extra flooring to order.
  • Subfloor prep. Uneven or damaged subfloors may need levelling or repair. This is not in the estimate.
  • Condo rules. Many condo buildings require underlayment for sound control and may limit work hours. See the soundproof flooring guide. Check your building's rules before you buy.
  • Removal. Taking out old flooring adds labour, and disposal of old material may cost extra.
  • Stairs. Stairs are not included in this estimate. Ask for a separate quote.
