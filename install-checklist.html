Engineered hardwood: what is different

Engineered hardwood is not always a floating floor. The steps above change in these ways:

  • Acclimation is usually longer than for laminate or vinyl. Follow the manufacturer's instructions.
  • Installation options include nail-down, glue-down, and sometimes floating. Which one you can use depends on the product and the subfloor.
  • Moisture limits for the subfloor and the room are stricter. Test, and stay within the manufacturer's limits.
  • Consider a professional install, especially for glue-down or nail-down jobs.
