Vinyl Wear Layer Guide: 12 mil vs 20 mil

The wear layer is the clear top layer on vinyl and SPC flooring. It protects the printed film that gives the floor its wood or stone look. A thicker wear layer takes more scuffs and wear before the print is affected.

It is measured in mils. 1 mil is 1/1000 of an inch, about 0.0254 mm. So 12 mil is about 0.3 mm and 20 mil is about 0.5 mm.

Wear layer thickness is not the same as plank thickness. A 6.5 mm plank can have a 12 mil or a 20 mil wear layer. The total thickness includes the core and any attached underlayment. The wear layer is only the clear top layer.

Other things that matter more than you think

  • Core type. SPC has a hard stone-plastic core. WPC has a softer, thicker core that feels warmer underfoot but dents more easily. SPC is usually the better pick for heavy furniture and busy rooms.
  • Total thickness. This is the full plank, not the wear layer. It affects stability and how well the floor hides small imperfections in the subfloor.
  • Attached underlayment (IXPE). Many planks come with a foam layer on the back for comfort and sound. Check whether it is included before you buy extra underlayment. See do you need underlayment for SPC vinyl.
  • AC rating. The AC rating is for laminate, not vinyl. Do not use it to compare vinyl. For laminate thickness, read 12mm vs 8mm laminate.
  • Top coating. Scratch resistance also depends on the surface coating on top of the wear layer. Two floors with the same mil number can wear differently.

FAQ

Is 20 mil worth it?

For kitchens, entries, hallways, rentals and homes with dogs, usually yes. It costs a little more and gives a bigger margin for wear. For a guest bedroom, 12 mil is often enough.

Is 12 mil good enough for a basement?

Often yes, if the basement is a quiet family room or bedroom without pets. Choose 20 mil if it is a play area, gym or rental suite. Moisture is a separate issue. Read the basement flooring guide.

Does wear layer affect waterproofing?

No. The core and the locking system decide how the floor handles water. A thicker wear layer does not make a floor more waterproof.

Can dogs scratch 20 mil?

It resists scratches better than 12 mil, but it is not scratch-proof. Long nails, grit and dragged furniture can still mark any floor. Keep nails trimmed and use felt pads.
