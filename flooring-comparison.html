Which one for which room

  • Basement: SPC or WPC vinyl. Waterproof planks handle damp air and small leaks better than wood. Test the slab for moisture first.
  • Kitchen: SPC vinyl. Waterproof and dent resistant. A water-resistant laminate or engineered hardwood can also work if you wipe spills fast.
  • Bathroom: SPC or WPC vinyl. Standing water and steam are hard on laminate and wood.
  • Condo: Laminate, SPC or engineered hardwood, with the underlayment your building requires. Check your condo's sound rules before you buy.
  • Bedroom: Engineered hardwood or WPC. Both feel warm and quiet. Laminate is a good lower-cost choice.
  • Living room: Engineered hardwood for a real wood look. Laminate or SPC if you want a tougher, lower-cost floor.
  • Homes with pets: SPC vinyl or a high-AC laminate. Both resist scratches, and SPC also handles accidents. Keep nails trimmed on any floor.
